Zaim Organizer Gmail

A personal utility operated by its owner to read receipt and order-confirmation emails and help review household ledger classifications using the owner's existing rules. It is not affiliated with Google or Zaim and is not offered as a public service.

Read-only Gmail access is requested because receipt details are contained in message bodies. The scheduled reader searches the last 100 days and retrieves at most 100 relevant messages per weekly run. It does not send, delete, label, or mark email as read.

Receipt retrieval produces an encrypted preview. Automatic use for ledger changes requires separately validated transaction matching and existing personal classification rules. No language-model API is used in scheduled processing.

See our privacy policy and terms. Contact the operator through the project issue tracker; do not post private data or credentials.
